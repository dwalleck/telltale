# 08: Baseline columns

**What to build:** With a list of baseline sprint IDs in configuration, the cycle time table gains baseline median and P85 columns and the time in status table gains a baseline median column, each pooled over all issues in the baseline sprints. Baseline figures come from their own metric views and follow the same suppression rule. With no baseline configured, the report is unchanged.

Owner decisions (2026-11-12): a baseline row is shown only when the pooled group passes the suppression rule (pooled team of at least five, at least three distinct assignees behind the row) and no baseline sprint has that row suppressed in its own view; otherwise its figures are withheld. A baseline sprint with no issue in a row does not suppress it. The pooled team is the distinct assignees of every issue of every baseline sprint. The baseline views carry no issue count. A baseline sprint ID is checked against the store at `derive` and `report`; the loader checks digits only (D44). Every row of a sprint that fails the five-person check counts as suppressed (D45). A sprint that fails the five-person check cannot be a baseline sprint: `derive` and `report` refuse the list and name the sprint (D47). This replaces D46, which accepted a suppressed baseline row appearing for such a sprint; the review showed that the pattern of baseline rows then gives back which of that sprint's buckets and statuses are non-empty. A baseline sprint must also be a completed sprint of the board. D50 (2026-11-12) changes what happens when a listed sprint fails the five-person check: nothing is refused and nothing stops; every baseline figure is withheld, and the console and the report name the sprint. A reassignment in Jira can take a fixed baseline sprint below five, and the owner should not have to edit a fixed list to get a report.
